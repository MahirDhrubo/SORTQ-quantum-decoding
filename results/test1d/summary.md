## Review (2026-11-12)
**Checked against the raw traces** (`p*_*.npz`, recomputed independently): fails, LER, paired counts, McNemar p,
iteration mean / p99 / p99.9 / max and non-converged counts all match the tables below. 0 syndrome mismatches.

**Corrections and additions:**
1. **"Iterations per window" must not be compared with global as "fewer iterations".** A window iteration covers
   W rounds; a global iteration covers all 37 detector rounds. Per shot, W6_C3 runs 12 windows × 36.5 = 438
   window-iterations vs 80.5 global. In round-iterations (iterations × rounds covered) they are similar: at
   p = 0.003, W6_C3 ≈ 12 × 36.5 × 6 ≈ 2,630 vs global ≈ 80.5 × 37 ≈ 2,980 (computed from the measured means;
   the last window is shorter, so W6_C3 is slightly lower than this).
2. **Direction differs between p values.** At p = 0.003 all windows have equal or fewer fails than global; at
   p = 0.002 all three have more (1.21–1.32×). None is significant at 0.05, but W6_C3 at p = 0.002 is borderline
   (p = 0.0505). More shots at p = 0.002 would be needed to say windowing costs nothing there.
3. **Non-converged = almost always failed.** Global: 17 / 17 (p = 0.003) and 12 / 12 (p = 0.002) shots with a
   non-converged decode failed. Windowed: 19 / 21, 12 / 15, 7 / 12 (p = 0.003) and 18 / 22, 10 / 26, 10 / 20
   (p = 0.002) — so in windows a non-converged window often does not cause a logical failure.
4. **Per-window means** (measured): bulk windows are flat across the circuit (e.g. W6_C3 p = 0.003: 36.9–41.6);
   the last window is short and cheap (9–11 at p = 0.003, 5–6 at p = 0.002).
5. **36-round vs 12-round LER (consistency check, not a prediction).** Using the measured 12-round Test 1b F32
   sweep LER and the per-round formula: p = 0.003 → expected 2.21e-3, measured 2.60e-3 ± 0.29e-3 (1.3 σ);
   p = 0.002 → expected 1.25e-4, measured 1.12e-4 ± 0.15e-4 (0.9 σ). Consistent. (The 1.8e-3 in the prep summary
   used the 12-round F64 Test 2b LER and was only for choosing shot counts.)
